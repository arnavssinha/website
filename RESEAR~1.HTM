Materials Science

Bacterial self-healing concrete

Cracked Labs · peer-reviewed student journal

Resolved a "soft-shell paradox" in existing self-healing concrete literature - soft polymer carriers weaken concrete 10–20%.

  • Engineered a diatomaceous-earth hard-shell carrier with a vacuum-impregnation protocol reaching ~85% pore-filling efficiency.
  • Controlled experiment across 12 specimens (28-day cure, 0.5mm cracks, 28-day healing) - ~62% crack closure via combined chemical + biological treatment.
