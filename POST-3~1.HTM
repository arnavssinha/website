Getting Started with Fusion 360 - Your First Robot Sketch

~2 min read View original on LinkedIn ↗

Okay. You get why CAD matters. Now actually do it.

Fusion 360 is free for students - sign up here with your school email. Install it. Open it. It looks overwhelming. That is normal.

Click "Create" > "New Sketch." Pick the XY plane. Blank canvas. Done. Find the Rectangle tool. Draw something about 3 inches by 2 inches. Do not worry about perfect.

It will be blue. That means Fusion has no idea where this thing is supposed to sit. Add dimensions. Hit the D key. Click a side. Type 3. Enter. Other side. Type 2. Now it is locked - 3 inches by 2 inches, exact.

Here is why this matters: in CAD there is no approximate. The machine expects 3 inches. Not close. Exactly 3.

Hit "Finish Sketch." Save it. Ctrl+S. Name it whatever. Fusion saves to the cloud. You designed something before building it. You know the exact size. You can change it without wasting materials. You can show people before you cut anything expensive.

Play around with it. Try different shapes. Change the dimensions. Watch what happens. You cannot break anything in CAD.
